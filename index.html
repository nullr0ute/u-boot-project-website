Welcome to U-Boot's website. U-Boot is a free and open-source boot loader available for many architectures, including ARM, MIPS, PowerPC, RISC-V, and x86.

There are many ways to get involved in the U-Boot community:

U-Boot custodian trees also have their own mailing list.
